AI prompts for Facebook and Instagram ads for your online store

Ad copy fails when it talks about you instead of the person scrolling. These prompts force the AI to start from the viewer's problem, then give you several angles to test instead of one "best" ad.

Copy-and-paste prompts

Replace every [BRACKET] with your own details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI assistant.

1. Five hooks to test

Write 5 different opening lines for a Facebook ad for [PRODUCT]. The audience is [AUDIENCE]. Use these angles, one each: a pain point, a surprising fact I can verify, a before-and-after, a question, a short story. Max 12 words per line.

2. Primary text, three lengths

Write the primary text for a Facebook ad for [PRODUCT] aimed at [AUDIENCE]. Give me a 1-line version, a 3-line version and a 6-line version. Lead with the problem, state one clear benefit, end with a single call to action: [CTA]. No exaggerated claims and no promises of results.

3. Headline variations

Give me 10 headline options under 40 characters for [PRODUCT]. Mix benefit headlines, curiosity headlines and offer headlines. Offer: [OFFER].

How to get better results

  • Test one thing at a time: hook first, then the offer, then the image.
  • Keep claims you can prove. Ad platforms reject exaggerated promises.
  • Use the customer's own words from reviews and support emails.
  • Write the call to action last, and make it a single action.

FAQ

How many ad variations should I test?

Start with 3 to 5 hooks on the same offer and image. Keep the winners and write new variations from them.

Does this work for TikTok too?

Yes. Use the hook prompt and read the line out loud as the first sentence of a video.
