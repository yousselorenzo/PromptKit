1. Ten hooks

Write 10 hooks for a 20-second UGC video about [PRODUCT] for [AUDIENCE]. The problem is [PAIN]. Use five styles: question, bold statement, "stop doing X", mini story, curiosity gap. Max 10 words each.

2. 20-second script

Write a 20-second UGC script for [PRODUCT]. Structure: hook (3 s), problem (4 s), product in use (8 s), result I can honestly show (3 s), call to action (2 s). Conversational, as if talking to a friend. Include on-screen text for each beat.

3. Shot list

Turn this script into a shot list for a phone: [PASTE SCRIPT]. For each shot give the framing, what is in the frame, and the line spoken. Keep it to 6 shots.

How to get better results

  • Film the hook last, once you know what the video shows.
  • Show the product in use inside the first 8 seconds.
  • Say only what you can show on camera.
  • Disclose partnerships as required by the platform.
