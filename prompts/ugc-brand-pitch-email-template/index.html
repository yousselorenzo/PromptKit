UGC brand pitch email: template and AI prompt

Brands get dozens of "I love your brand, let's work together" emails a week. The ones that get replies are short, specific and show proof. Use this structure and let the AI do the first draft.

Copy-and-paste prompts

Replace every [BRACKET] with your own details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI assistant.

1. Pitch email draft

Write a cold email from a UGC creator to [BRAND]. Structure: one specific line about their product or recent campaign, one line of proof from my work [PROOF: views, niche, sample], a clear offer [OFFER: 3 videos in 7 days], and a single question to end. Under 100 words, plain tone, subject line under 45 characters.

2. Three subject lines

Give me 10 subject lines under 45 characters for a UGC pitch to [BRAND]. Mix curiosity, a named result I can prove, and a direct offer. No clickbait and no all-caps.

3. Follow-up after 5 days

Write a 40-word follow-up to my pitch to [BRAND]. Add one new useful idea: [IDEA, e.g. a hook for their new product]. Do not say "just checking in".

How to get better results

  • Find the right contact: the brand's social or partnerships manager.
  • Attach one sample, not a whole portfolio.
  • Send 5 tailored pitches rather than 50 identical ones.
  • Quote real numbers only. Never claim results you cannot show.

FAQ

How long should a pitch be?

Under 100 words. The goal of the first email is a reply, not a contract.

When should I follow up?

After about five days, with something new in the message.
