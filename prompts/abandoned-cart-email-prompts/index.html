Abandoned cart email prompts: a 3-email sequence

Many shoppers leave without buying and never come back. A short, helpful sequence brings some of them back. These prompts write the three emails, each with a different job.

Copy-and-paste prompts

Replace every [BRACKET] with your own details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI assistant.

1. Email 1: the reminder (1 hour later)

Write a short abandoned cart email for [STORE] selling [PRODUCT]. Job: remind the shopper what they left, make checkout one click away, and offer help. Warm, plain tone, under 80 words, subject line under 40 characters. No fake urgency.

2. Email 2: answer the doubt (next day)

Write an abandoned cart email that answers the most common doubts about [PRODUCT]: [DOUBTS, e.g. sizing, shipping time, returns]. Use a short FAQ format with 3 questions. Include our return policy: [POLICY]. Subject line under 40 characters.

3. Email 3: last call (day 3)

Write a final abandoned cart email for [PRODUCT]. If an incentive is allowed, mention only this real offer: [OFFER OR "none"]. Be honest about any deadline. Under 70 words, one button text.

How to get better results

  • Only mention discounts and deadlines that are real.
  • Show the product image and the exact item left in the cart.
  • Send the first email within a few hours, the rest over 2 to 3 days.
  • Add an unsubscribe link and follow your local email rules.

Do I need a discount in the sequence?
