ChatGPT prompts for real estate listings and follow-ups

"Spacious home in a great location" tells buyers nothing. These prompts make the AI describe how the home is lived in, stay clear of language that breaks fair housing rules, and write the follow-up that keeps a lead warm.

Copy-and-paste prompts

Replace every [BRACKET] with your own details, then paste into ChatGPT, Claude or any AI assistant.

1. Listing description

Write a listing description for [PROPERTY: beds, baths, size, key features]. Describe how a buyer would use the spaces day to day. Mention [NEARBY: park, school, transit] only as distances or facts. Do not describe the ideal buyer or the neighborhood's people, and avoid any wording that could breach fair housing rules. 150 words.

2. Open house post

Write an Instagram caption and a Facebook post for an open house at [PROPERTY] on [DATE AND TIME]. Lead with one concrete detail that makes the home different. End with the address and time. Add 5 relevant hashtags.

3. Lead follow-up email

Write a warm follow-up email to a buyer who toured [PROPERTY] two days ago. Mention one thing they said they liked: [DETAIL]. Offer two next steps: [OPTIONS]. Under 100 words, no pressure.

How to get better results

  • Describe features and distances, not who should live there.
  • Lead with the one detail photos cannot show.
  • Check every fact against the MLS entry before posting.
  • Follow up inside 48 hours while the tour is fresh.

FAQ

Is AI-written listing copy allowed?

Check your brokerage and MLS rules. You stay responsible for accuracy and for fair housing compliance, so read and edit every listing.

Can AI replace my follow-up?

It drafts the message. Personal details from the tour are what make it work.
